=== Extension name ===
YouTube Comment Timestamp Chapters

=== Short description (max 132 characters) ===
Marks timestamps mentioned in YouTube comments right on the video progress bar, like chapters.

=== Category ===
Productivity  (Video/Movies also fits if offered)

=== Detailed description ===
Ever seen a YouTube comment like "4:48 — I love you too 😭✌️" and wished you
could jump straight there without scrubbing through the whole video? This
extension finds those timestamps automatically and turns them into visual
markers on the video's own progress bar — like chapters, but crowdsourced
from the comments section.

FEATURES

• Progress bar markers — every timestamp mentioned in the comments gets a
  tick mark on the scrubber. Mentioned by multiple commenters? It turns gold.
• Hover tooltip — hover a marker to see the timestamp and a preview of the
  comment that mentioned it.
• "Coming up" toast — a heads-up a few seconds before playback reaches a
  marker, so you don't miss the moment. The lead time grows automatically
  for longer comments so you have time to read them. Hover it to pause and
  read at your own pace; click the X to dismiss it.
• Optional chime — plays when a marker is coming up, with its own volume
  slider (0–150%) and support for uploading your own short sound clip.
• Fully customizable — every feature above can be toggled off independently,
  and the toast's on-screen position (6 placements) is configurable.

PRIVACY

This extension reads the YouTube page you're already viewing to find
timestamp links in the comments — it doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any
data. All settings (including any custom sound clip you upload) are stored
locally in your own browser via chrome.storage.local and never leave your
machine. See the privacy policy for details.
