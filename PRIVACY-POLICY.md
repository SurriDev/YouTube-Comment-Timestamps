# Privacy Policy — YouTube Comment Timestamp Chapters

**Last updated:** 2026-08-02

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data,
browsing history, or analytics of any kind.

## What the extension does

- It reads the YouTube video page you are already viewing (comment text and
  the video player) to find timestamps mentioned in comments and display
  them as markers on the video's progress bar.
- All settings you configure (which features are enabled, sound volume,
  toast position, and any custom sound clip you upload) are stored **only**
  in your own browser, using the standard `chrome.storage.local` API.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not send any data to any server. There is no backend.
- It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.
- It does not read or store your browsing history outside of youtube.com
  pages.
- A custom sound clip you upload stays on your device; it is never
  uploaded anywhere.

## Permissions

- `storage`: used only to save your settings locally in your own browser
  profile.
